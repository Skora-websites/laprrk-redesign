## 10. Inconsistencies and issues to resolve with the client

1. **Headline counts disagree across pages.** Home says 19+ client engagements, 76+ engineers
   placed, 189+ trained. About says 50+ clients, 200+ placed, 500+ trained. Hiring says 200+ placed.
   Training says 500+ trained. The new site uses the About/Hiring/Training figures (50+/200+/500+)
   because they are the ones repeated on the practice pages and in the case studies. **Confirm
   before launch.**
2. **A stray "763"** sits above the "Operating since" caption on the home page, apparently a
   template artefact.
3. **Service counts are inconsistent:** "18 tracks · 10 programmes" in one place, "18 role-based
   tracks" in another, 17 IT services, 10 hiring services. The new site states 17 / 10 / 18.
4. **Contact page has a form-shaped gap.** "Book a free consultation" on the contact page links to
   the contact page. This is fixed in the redesign: a real form that opens WhatsApp with a
   pre-composed message.
5. **Blog dates are in the future relative to capture** (Sep 2026 posts, three only). The redesign
   does not carry a blog section forward; if articles are wanted, they need an archive page and
   regular publication.
6. **"16 technology groups" is claimed but only 8 are shown** on the home page. The roles page is
   linked for the rest.
7. **No pricing ranges anywhere**, only pricing models. The redesign adds an engagement-model table
   (still no rates, which is appropriate for this kind of firm).
8. **No team page, no client logos, no testimonials.** Deliberate in the original (client names are
   withheld), but it weakens trust; see the design plan's proof strategy.
9. **Typography and icon inconsistencies:** three different glyph sets (◈ ✦ ⬢ ✧ ⬣ ❖), mixed
   "5 to 7" and "5–7", "Trainings for IT corporates" as a link label.
10. **SEO:** each page has a unique title, but no structured data, and the home page's H1 competes
    with a very long title tag. The redesign supplies a single descriptive title, meta description
    and Open Graph tags.
